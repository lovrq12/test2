HOW TO PLAY
🎯 OBJECTIVE
Citizens must find and eliminate the Mafia before the Mafia eliminates everyone. The Mafia wins by outnumbering the Citizens.
🌑 NIGHT PHASE
Everyone closes their eyes. The Mafia secretly chooses a victim. The Doctor secretly chooses someone to save. The Detective secretly investigates a player.
☀️ DAY PHASE
The eliminated player is revealed. Everyone discusses and votes to eliminate a suspect. The player with the most votes is eliminated.
